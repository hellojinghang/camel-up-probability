LEARNING NOTE

Probability is a range of possibilities.

A 70% chance still loses about three times in ten. Judge a decision by what was knowable at the time, not by a single outcome.

Exercises use classic rules, fixed tiles and fair dice. In ticket-value mode, compare the displayed payouts as well as the odds.

How the calculations work

Fair dice and equally likely remaining die selection are assumed. Spectator tiles stay fixed during a leg. Second edition rolls five of six dice; one remains in the pyramid. The race stops immediately at a finish-line crossing, including a Crazy Camel crossing backwards.

Expected value (EV) = first-place payout × P(1st) + P(2nd) − P(3rd, 4th or 5th). This is the expected change in game coins for one ticket. It does not model opponents, future turns, opportunity cost, or a complete optimal strategy.

For second edition, the legacy White racing slot is displayed as Purple, distinct from Crazy White. The internal identifier remains compatible with the original engine.
